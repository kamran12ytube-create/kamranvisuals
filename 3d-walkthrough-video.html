3D Walkthrough Videos — Walk Through Your Home Before It’s Built

A 3D walkthrough video lets you walk through your house before it is built: arrive at the gate, step through the front door and see how every room opens up. A drone flythrough rendering shows the whole building and its site from the air.

Architectural walkthrough animation is planned around how a home is actually used — arrival, movement and the key rooms — with music, titles and your logo. A 60-second walkthrough, modeling included, starts at $990.

House walkthrough videos and drone flythroughs

An interior walkthrough follows a path through the rooms at eye level. An exterior drone flythrough orbits or approaches the building to show the facade, garden and surroundings. Most videos combine a short exterior opening with an interior walk.

A virtual tour before building

A walkthrough is the closest thing to visiting your home before it exists. Families use it to agree on the layout and finishes; builders and developers use it to sell homes before construction starts.

Planned before production

Every video starts with an agreed camera path drawn over the plan, so you know what the video will show before rendering begins. Videos are delivered in 16:9 for websites and presentations, and in 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp status.

What to send

  • Floor plans, elevations and sections, or an existing 3D model
  • Material and furniture references for each space shown
  • The route through the house and the rooms to highlight
  • Target length, and 16:9 and/or 9:16 format
  • Logo, contact details and any text to appear on screen
  • Music preference (licensed tracks only)

What you receive

  • Camera path for approval before rendering
  • Final MP4 video in HD or 4K as agreed
  • 16:9 and/or 9:16 versions
  • Optional short social cut and still frames
  • Two rounds of changes included

FAQ

Questions about a walkthrough video

How much does a 3D walkthrough cost?

A 60-second walkthrough video, modeling included, is $990. Extra length is $15 per second. The Walkthrough package — a floor plan, 5 renders and a 60-second video — is $1,290.

How long does a walkthrough take?

Most walkthrough videos are delivered in 7–14 days, depending on length and detail.

Can I get a vertical version for Instagram or TikTok?

Yes. A 9:16 version is planned from the start, which works better than cropping a horizontal video later.

Can you add music and my logo?

Yes. Your logo and contact details can be added, with properly licensed music or music you supply.
