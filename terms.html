Standard project terms

Unless your quote says otherwise:

  • Payment: 50% to start and 50% before the final files are delivered. Previews are watermarked until the final payment.
  • Changes: two rounds of changes are included. Extra rounds are $49 each. Changes to the design after approval, or new items, are quoted before work continues.
