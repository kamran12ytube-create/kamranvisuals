Concept Project

Stone Courtyard House

Aerial Exterior · Residential

A two-story stone house wrapped around an olive-tree courtyard and pool, presented as an aerial model view.

Overview

Seen from above, the house reads as a series of pale stone volumes with timber screens, arranged around a courtyard with a mature olive tree and a pool. Glazed living spaces open onto the courtyard, the upper floor has planted roof terraces, and low stepped walls with warm uplighting define the garden edges.

Design objective

Explain the massing, the courtyard layout and the relationship between house, pool and garden in one clear overview image.
