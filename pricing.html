What affects the quote

What can change the final price

Complexity
Detailed facades, custom furniture and large sites take longer than simple forms.
Number of views or rooms
Each additional camera view, room or unit type adds production time.
Source material
Clear, dimensioned drawings are faster to work from than sketches that need interpretation.
Video duration
Longer animations need more scenes, rendering and editing.
Resolution and formats
Very large print sizes, 4K video or several aspect ratios add work.
Revisions
Two rounds of changes are included; extra rounds are $49 each.
Deadline
Rush delivery in half the normal time is available for +30%.

FAQ

Questions about prices

How much does 3D rendering cost?

Interior renders are $249 per view, exterior renders $299 per view and aerial or drone views $399. Most clients choose the Visualize package: a furnished 3D floor plan plus 3 renders for $599.

How much does a floor plan design cost?

A clean 2D floor plan redraw is $39 per floor and a furnished 3D floor plan is $99 per floor (up to 2,500 sq ft). Real estate agents can get 10 branded plans a month for $690.

How much does a 3D walkthrough cost?

A 60-second walkthrough video, modeling included, is $990. Extra length is $15 per second. The Walkthrough package adds a floor plan and 5 renders for $1,290.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. You know the price before work starts. Two rounds of changes are included; extra rounds ($49), rush delivery (+30%) and source files (+$99) are only added if you ask for them.

Scope note: Visualizations are created for design communication and presentation. They are not construction or permit documents.
