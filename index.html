FAQ

Questions About 3D Rendering

Scope note: Visualizations are created for design communication and presentation. They are not construction or permit documents.

How much does 3D rendering cost?

A furnished 3D floor plan starts at $99 per floor, interior renders at $249 per view, exterior renders at $299 per view and a 60-second walkthrough video at $990. Packages start at $99. See the pricing page for every price.

What files do I need to send?

Whatever you have: a hand sketch, a PDF, a CAD file (DWG/DXF), a SketchUp or Revit model, or photos of a drawing — plus reference photos of the look you want.

How long does it take?

Floor plans take 2–3 days, renders 3–5 days and walkthrough videos 7–14 days. Rush delivery in half the time is available for +30%.

Can I make changes?

Yes. Two rounds of changes are included in every order. Extra rounds are $49 each.

Do you work with clients in the US and other countries?

Yes. I work with clients all over the world, most of them in the United States, as well as the UAE and the Gulf, the UK, Canada, Australia and Pakistan. My studio is based in Dubai, and everything runs online by email and WhatsApp. Files are shared through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% before the final files. Previews are watermarked until the final payment.

Can you work from a hand sketch or a photo of a drawing?

Yes. A clear sketch with key measurements becomes an accurate plan. Without reliable measurements, the result is presented as an illustrative layout, and the quote says so.

Are your plans construction drawings?

No. Plans and visuals are concept designs for planning, presenting and selling. For permits, a locally licensed architect or engineer stamps the final construction drawings.
